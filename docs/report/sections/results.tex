\subsection{CNN Training Curves}

The two figures below show the nine epochs of the final CNN run. Training loss falls in every epoch, from 1.549 to 0.766, and training accuracy rises from 0.728 to 0.992. Validation loss falls from 1.086 to a minimum of 0.810 at epoch 9, but validation accuracy and macro-F1 peak at epoch 6, both at 0.9686, and are lower afterwards. The widening gap between training and validation accuracy after epoch 6 is the overfitting that early stopping is meant to catch, and the checkpoint from epoch 6 was kept. The run stopped after epoch 9 because the validation macro-F1 had not improved for three consecutive epochs.
